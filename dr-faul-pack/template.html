An engineering studio, not an agency

We build working systems for South African businesses. Not proofs of concept, not slide decks, and not software that makes the business reshape itself around it.

What we believe

  • Evidence before build. No engagement opens with an assumption about your business. The audit comes first, and it is allowed to conclude that an idea is not worth building.
  • Acceleration, not just automation. Automation removes a task. Acceleration changes what the business is capable of. We aim for the second.
  • Security is not a closing phase. Row-level access control, hardened authentication and zero-trust principles are in place from the first commit, not bolted on at handover.
  • Skilled hours belong on skilled work. If an experienced person spends their week on reporting, reconciliation and routing, that is an engineering problem.
  • You own what we build. Intellectual property in every system, asset and piece of content transfers to you on full settlement. No lock-in to a platform we own.

How we are different

Most studios sell either strategy or hands. We do both, so the person who scopes the work is the person who architects it and the person who answers your email about it. Our delivery model favours AI-augmented engineering over conventional development cycles, and that compression is passed to the client as speed and as cost, not kept as margin.

The Foundational AI Audit

Every engagement begins here. Before any commitment to build, we establish where applied AI will produce the clearest return, and equally where it will not.

What we examine

  • Data availability and quality. What exists, where it lives, how clean it is, and whether it can be reached programmatically.
  • Integration points. How existing systems talk to each other, and where they do not.
  • Workflow analysis. Which steps consume disproportionate skilled hours relative to the judgement they need.
  • Decision latency. The gap between an event and a decision on it, usually the most expensive number in the business.
  • Security posture. Authentication, access control and exposure across connected assets.
  • Compliance readiness. POPIA obligations attaching to the data in scope.

What you receive

A prioritised roadmap

Every opportunity scored for impact, effort and risk, sequenced so the earliest work funds and de-risks the later work.

A defensible cost model

Indicative investment attached to each item, so the roadmap can go to a board or budget process without translation.

An explicit "do not build" list

The items where applied AI will not return, and why. Often the most valuable page in the report.

A security and compliance summary

What is exposed today and what the remediation sequence looks like.

The audit stands alone

The roadmap is yours on delivery. You are under no obligation to implement it with us, and we would rather hand over an honest roadmap you take elsewhere than sell a build we cannot defend. Audits are custom-scoped, and investment is confirmed in writing before the work begins.

Hermes: automation that acts

Hermes is our autonomous agent framework, configured per business. It is the studio's flagship, and the clearest expression of acceleration rather than automation.

Hermes runs asynchronous subagents. Instead of working through a queue one task at a time, it monitors incoming information, prepares scheduled work, compiles reporting and routes communication concurrently. Configured properly, it absorbs the administrative load carried by experienced people and returns that time to the work only they can do.

What Hermes handles today

  • Financial administration. Expense capture, categorisation, VAT treatment and tax-year summaries.
  • Invoicing and quoting. Generation, numbering, dispatch and follow-up on what is unpaid.
  • Inbox operations. Triage, labelling, drafting, and escalation of what truly needs a human.
  • Calendar and scheduling. Coordination, preparation briefs and conflict resolution.
  • Documents and files. Structured storage, naming discipline and retrieval across cloud drives.
  • Reporting. Scheduled management reports from live data, not a monthly scramble.

Conversational and voice agents. Alongside back-office agents we build customer-facing agents, including WhatsApp agents for hospitality and service businesses, where the customer never leaves the channel they already use. Voice agents are an active development track.

Autonomy without control is a liability. So every agent ships with a contract.

Guardrails and control

Written specification

Exact field mappings, controlled vocabulary and explicit limits, not loose instructions.

Approval gates

Money, new recipients and destructive actions stay behind human confirmation.

Supervised run-in

Confirmation is removed gradually. Logging and anomaly alerts stay permanent.

AI cybersecurity, not an afterthought

An agent with access to your inbox, calendar and invoicing holds a serious privilege. Security is engineered into every build, with a dedicated monitoring layer on top.

  • Least privilege by default. Every agent touches only what its job requires.
  • Credentials scoped per integration. Never pooled, never shared across clients.
  • Approval gates on consequence. Anything money-related or ambiguous escalates to a human.
  • Continuous monitoring. Dedicated scanning through our Aikido Security partnership.
  • Codebase and dependency audits. Reviewed on a cycle, not left to accumulate risk.
  • POPIA-conscious data handling. Built for South African compliance expectations.
